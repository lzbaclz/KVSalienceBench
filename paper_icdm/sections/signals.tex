\section{Signals and empirical feature selection}
\label{sec:signals}

\subsection{Recorded views}
The feature family contains a normalized within-layer attention EMA
$x_{\rm wl}=\bar a_{\ell,b,t}/\max_{b'}\bar a_{\ell,b',t}$, a previous-layer
hot-block indicator $x_{\rm cl}=\mathbf 1[\bar a_{\ell-1,b,t}\text{ is in the top-}r]$,
a query-derived proxy $x_{\rm q}=(1+\cos(\tilde q_t,\tilde K_{\ell,b}))/2$ and a
temporal-age feature $x_{\rm age}=\exp(-(t-p_b)/w)$.
Here $\bar a$ is an EMA and $p_b$ the block-creation step supplied by the collector,
so $x_{\rm age}$ is an age proxy, not an LRU access-recency measurement; the tildes
mark the archived query representation, whose coordinate-space audit in
\S\ref{sec:limits} limits conclusions about that view. The cross-layer signal is an
attention-magnitude carry-over, not InfiniGen's query-based mechanism; the offline
extractor uses the current layer's own EMA as its first-layer fallback, whereas
runtime realizations need their own explicit fallback and alignment rules
(Table~\ref{tab:protocol}).

\subsection{Relevance and interaction diagnostics}
\label{sec:redundancy}
We estimate relevance $I(X_i;Z)$ and interaction information
\[
\mathrm{II}(X_i;X_j;Z)=I(X_i;X_j\mid Z)-I(X_i;X_j)
\]
with quantile-binned plug-in estimators. On the version-2 corpus, within-layer
EMA and the cross-layer indicator have $I(X_i;Z)$ of 0.104 and 0.099, versus
0.007 for the cosine query proxy and under 0.001 for age
(Fig.~\ref{fig:redundcalib}); raw-probe AUCs are in Table~\ref{tab:v2}.
We omit the historical single-view fitted/raw AUC comparison because its sample
and aggregation correspondence cannot be certified: a positive monotone fit
alone preserves AUC. The signs of small interaction estimates are
\emph{descriptive}: small pairwise interaction implies neither conditional
independence nor the absence of higher-order effects.

Archived conditional-information diagnostics $U_i=I(X_i;Z\mid X_{-i})$, explored across
binning grids and label permutations, are larger for the magnitude views than for
the remaining proxies ($\le0.006$): within-layer is $0.133$--$0.155$ on every grid,
and the binary cross-layer estimate is $0.048$--$0.084$ on the finer grids but
collapses to zero under the coarsest one, so we report its grid sensitivity rather
than the most favorable cell. These estimates motivate a candidate subset, evaluated on
held-out requests; they are not a sufficiency certificate, and the evidence for the
compact model is its measured predictive performance.
